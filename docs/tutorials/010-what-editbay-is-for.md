Editbay edits video: the cutting, arranging and mixing that most editing actually is, done quickly.

## Steps

1. Bring your recordings in by dropping them on the window.
2. Cut them on the multi-track timeline, and stack pictures over one another. {show: timeline-tracks}
3. Mix the sound and add captions.
4. Export in whatever shape you need with `Ctrl+E`. {show: open-export}

## Details

What it leaves out matters as much as what it has. There are transitions, looks, text and
effects, but no speed ramps, no LUTs and no full colour grading. If you need a colour grade,
this is not the tool.

It is especially good at footage of someone talking, because it can find and remove every pause
for you, and because it writes captions on your own machine.

Everything happens locally. There is no account and no subscription, and the only time Editbay
touches the network at all is the one-off download of the speech model, the first time you ask
for captions.
