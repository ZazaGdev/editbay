Get your video, sound and image files into Sources, ready to cut.

## Steps

1. Drop video, sound or image files anywhere in the window. {show: bin-column}
2. Wait for each card in Sources to say it is ready. You can carry on working meanwhile. {show: asset-status on:asset}
3. Press [Add to timeline] on a card, or drag the card onto a track. {show: add-to-timeline on:asset}

## Details

Files you drop land in Sources, down the left side. Each one is prepared in the background: a
playable copy if the file needs one, a waveform, and a filmstrip. Large or awkward files take
longer.

You do not need to think about what the file is. Variable frame rate screen recordings, HEVC off
a phone, 4K with rotation metadata, several different frame rates in one project: all of it is
sorted out on the way in, so your cut stays frame accurate later instead of drifting.

If you record with OBS, your newest capture is already offered on the empty start screen.
Editbay reads OBS's own settings to find your recording folder, so there is nothing to set up.

To sort and filter Sources, see Finding things in Sources. For folders you use in every project,
such as sound effects, see Your own folders in Sources.
