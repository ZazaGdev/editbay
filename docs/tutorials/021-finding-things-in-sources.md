Keep a long list of sources easy to look through.

## Steps

1. Press a heading such as Today or Yesterday to shut or open it. {show: bin-group-toggle on:days}
2. Choose how to sort the sources. {show: bin-sort on:many}
3. Press [Video], [Sound] or [Image] to show only that kind. {show: bin-kind on:many}
4. Choose cards, a compact list or a grid of thumbnails with the three view buttons. {show: bin-view}

## Details

Once Sources holds a few days' work it folds into Today, Yesterday, This week and Earlier, and
each heading shuts.

Sorting, and showing only [Video], [Sound] or [Image], appear once there are more than five
sources. [All] shows every kind again.

The three buttons above the list show the sources as cards, with everything on them, as a
compact list, one line each, or as a grid of thumbnails. Double click a line or a thumbnail to
put it on the timeline.

To find something by name, use the search box at the top of Sources: see One search for all your
sources.
