Four parts: Sources, the monitor, the Inspector and the timeline.

## Steps

1. Sources, down the left, holds every recording you have brought in. {show: bin-column}
2. The monitor, in the middle, shows what you are looking at. Press `V` to swap its Source and Output views. {show: view}
3. The Inspector, down the right, has everything about the clip you have selected. {show: inspector-column}
4. The timeline, across the bottom, is the cut itself. Drag the divider above it to make it taller. {show: timeline-divider}

## Details

Sources is searchable and sortable. The dividers between the columns drag, and both Sources and
the Inspector fold away when you want the picture bigger. Dragging the divider above the timeline
up grows the timeline until the monitor's picture is small but still there, with its controls;
the height stays as you leave it, even with only a track or two. Double click it to put it back.

The monitor has two views. [Source] shows the whole recording with a crop box over it, and that
box says which part of the recording you are using. [Output] shows the finished frame. On a
vertical sequence there is room for both, so it shows them side by side and `V` chooses which
one the keys act on.

Selecting a clip leaves the playhead and the timeline where they are. Once you start framing it,
in the Inspector, on the monitor or with the nudge keys, the playhead goes into that clip, so what
you frame is always the clip you selected.

A ? beside a control says more about it when you point at it, and opens the tutorial that explains
it when you click it.
