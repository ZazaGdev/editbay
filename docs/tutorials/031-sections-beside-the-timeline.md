Keep an Inspector section, such as Sound, in reach while you cut.

## Steps

1. Drag a section of the Inspector by its heading. {show: inspector-group-heading on:picture}
2. Let go over the strip that opens at the left or right end of the timeline. {show: timeline-panel}
3. Or right click its heading and choose [Put left of the timeline] or [Put right of the timeline]. {show: inspector-group-heading on:picture "Right click a section heading, then choose where to put it"}
4. To put it back, drag it onto the Inspector or choose [Take away from beside the timeline]. {show: timeline-panel "Right click the section heading beside the timeline"}

## Details

Any section of the Inspector, such as Picture in picture or Sound, can sit in a small column to
the left or the right of the timeline. Pressing `Shift+F10` on its heading opens the same menu as
a right click. With several on one side, the menu also has [Move up] and [Move down].

Several sections on one side share its height; drag the line between two to give one more room,
and drag the side's edge to make it wider. Double click either to put it back. When the window is
too narrow for both a side and the timeline, the side folds to a thin rail; its button opens it.

For a taller Inspector, click [Fold down] at the foot of the sections right of the timeline. They
fold to a thin bar under the Inspector, which takes their height; click the bar to open them again.

A section you put beside the timeline is unticked in the Inspector, so it is not shown twice; see
Choose what the Inspector shows. Where you put them is kept for you, the same in every project.
