Hide the Inspector sections you do not use, or show just one.

## Steps

1. Press [All sections] in the Inspector's heading. {show: section-picker}
2. Untick a section to hide it there. {show: section-picker "Press this, then untick a section"}
3. Or choose [Only this] beside a section to show it alone. {show: section-picker "Press this, then Only this beside a section"}

## Details

The Inspector keeps every section; the list only chooses which are shown. Once some are hidden
the button says how many of the sections are shown, and [Show all sections] in its list brings them all back.

A section the selected clip does not have is greyed and marked not for this clip; you can still
tick it, for the clips that have it.

A section you put beside the timeline is unticked for you, so it is not shown twice. Tick it again
to have it in both places, where each folds on its own. Drag it back onto the Inspector, or choose
[Take away from beside the timeline], and it is ticked again. What you choose is kept, the same in
every project.
