Pick one of three looks for the window.

## Steps

1. Open [Settings]. {show: open-settings}
2. Under [General], choose a [Theme]. {show: open-settings "Open Settings, then General, then Theme"}

## Details

The themes are [Classic blue], the look the app starts with, [Arctic teal], and [Electric deep],
near-black with strong borders.

The window loads again in the one you choose; your project stays open.
