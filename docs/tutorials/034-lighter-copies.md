Stop playback stuttering on big recordings, without losing quality in the export.

## Steps

1. Open [Settings]. {show: open-settings}
2. Under [Monitor], turn on [Play lighter copies of recordings, for smoother playback]. {show: open-settings "Open Settings, then Monitor"}

## Details

The app makes a small copy of each recording on the timeline in the background and plays that in
the monitor as soon as it is ready. Everything looks and lines up the same, just lighter to play.

The export always uses your recordings themselves, so nothing you export is any lower in quality.
