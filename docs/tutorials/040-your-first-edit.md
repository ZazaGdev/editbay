From a recording to an exported video in four steps.

## Steps

1. Bring it in: drop a file in the window, then press [Add to timeline] on its card. {show: add-to-timeline on:asset}
2. Cut it down: move the playhead, press `S` to split, and delete what you do not want. {show: timeline-tracks}
3. Frame it: select a clip, then drag the crop box in the Source view. {show: view}
4. Send it out: press `Ctrl+E`. {show: open-export}

## Details

**Use only part of a recording.** Before adding it, press the card's ... button and choose
[Skim] to look through it first: move the pointer across the strip, press `I` where the good part
starts and `O` where it ends, then press the add button. Only the marked part goes on the
timeline. To use all of it, press [Add to timeline], or drag the card down onto a track.

**Trim instead of splitting.** Put the playhead where the clip should start and press `I`, where
it should end and press `O`.

**Framing.** The crop box in the Source view chooses what part of the recording fills the frame.
Press `V` and drag the picture in the Output view to place it.
