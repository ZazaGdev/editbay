Undo any change to the edit, and come back to a project exactly where you stopped.

## Steps

1. Press `Ctrl+Z` to undo. {show: undo}
2. Press `Ctrl+Y` to redo. {show: redo}
3. Reopen a project to carry on where you stopped. {show: recent-toggle}

## Details

Undo and redo cover everything about the edit. Where you are looking is never an edit, so
scrolling, zooming and moving the playhead do not fill your undo history with things you did not
mean to record.

The project file remembers where you were, not only what you cut: the playhead, the zoom, which
sequence you were on, and how tall the tracks were. Reopening a project puts you back exactly
where you stopped.
