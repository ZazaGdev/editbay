See how much room Editbay's copies of your recordings take, and free it.

## Steps

1. Open [Settings], then [Sources]. {show: open-settings "Open Settings, then Sources"}
2. [Media cache] shows how much room the copies take. {show: open-settings "Open Settings, then Sources, then Media cache"}
3. Press [Free the space other projects use]. {show: open-settings "Open Settings, then Sources, then Media cache"}

## Details

To play your recordings smoothly, Editbay makes copies of them: a version the preview can play, a
copy of each extra audio track, waveforms, filmstrips, cleaned or enhanced sound, and the pictures
of your texts and shapes. They live in Editbay's own media cache, never beside your files, and
your recordings are never changed.

Copies no project has used for two months are removed on their own when Editbay starts. Opening a
project marks its copies as used, so a project you come back to keeps them.

[Free the space other projects use] removes everything the open project does not use, straight
away. Nothing of yours is lost: another project makes its copies again the next time you open it,
which takes a little while for long recordings. A copy whose recording is no longer where it was
imported from, deleted or on a drive that is not plugged in, is the only copy of that footage, so
it is always kept, as is anything still being imported.
