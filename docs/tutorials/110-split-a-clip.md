Cut a clip in two at the playhead.

## Steps

1. Select the clip on the timeline. {show: clip on:picture}
2. Put the playhead on the frame where you want the cut. {show: playhead-head}
3. Press `S`. Or right click the clip and choose [Split here]. {show: clip on:picture "Right click this clip, then choose Split here"}
4. To cut through every track at that frame instead, press `Shift+S`.

## Details

`S` splits the selected clip in two at the playhead. `Shift+S` splits **every track** at that
frame, not just the selected clip. Useful when you want to cut straight through a stack of
picture and sound at the same moment.

The two halves stay linked to their sound, so splitting a clip splits its audio with it and the
pieces move together afterwards.

To find the moment first, `Up` and `Down` jump the playhead to the previous and next cut on any
track, so you can walk an edit without hunting with the mouse. `Left` and `Right` move one frame
at a time, and with `Shift` held, one second.

To put the pieces back together, see Join the pieces of a split clip. To make pieces act as one
clip without bringing anything back, see Group clips into one, keeping the cuts.
