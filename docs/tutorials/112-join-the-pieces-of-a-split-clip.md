Put a split clip back together without undoing everything since.

## Steps

1. Right click either piece on the timeline. {show: clip on:picture}
2. Choose [Join], then [With the next clip] or [With the previous clip]. {show: clip on:picture "Right click this clip, then choose Join"}
3. Or select the pieces and press `Ctrl+J`.

## Details

With several pieces selected, `Ctrl+J` joins them all; with one, it joins at the edge nearest the
playhead. The sound joins with its picture, and the joined clip keeps the first piece's settings.

**Where something was taken out between the pieces**, as Remove silences leaves them, Join brings
it back. It asks first, saying how much comes back ("brings back 1.2 s"), since the sequence gets
that much longer; the right click item says it too. One undo takes it all back.

Two pieces of different recordings, or a second piece that plays earlier footage than the first
ends on, can never be one clip.

To make pieces act as one clip without bringing anything back, group them instead: see Group
clips into one, keeping the cuts.
