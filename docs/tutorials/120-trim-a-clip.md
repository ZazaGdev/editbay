Set where a clip starts and ends, by the playhead or by dragging its edges.

## Steps

1. Select the clip on the timeline. {show: clip on:picture}
2. Put the playhead where it should start and press `I`. {show: playhead-head}
3. Put the playhead where it should end and press `O`.
4. Or drag either end of the clip instead. {show: trim-end on:picture}
5. Hold `Alt` while you drag to turn snapping off for that one drag.

## Details

**By the playhead** is the accurate way, because you are watching the exact frame as you set it.

**By dragging** is quicker. Holding `Alt` while you drag turns snapping off for that one
movement, which you want when a snap keeps pulling you to a neighbouring edge you do not care
about. To turn it off for good, press `N` or the [Snap] button above the timeline; the same again
turns it back on.

A clip cannot be trimmed past the footage it has. If there is nothing left of the recording at
that end, the trim stops there rather than inventing frames.

On V1, dragging an edge moves everything after the clip with it: see Trim on V1, where the rest
follows.
