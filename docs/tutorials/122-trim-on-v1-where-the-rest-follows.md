Drag a clip's edge on V1 and everything after it moves to match, with no gap left behind.

## Steps

1. Drag either end of a clip on V1. {show: trim-end on:picture}
2. Drag it out to bring back footage past the cut, or in to shorten the clip.
3. Hold `Ctrl` while you drag to trim only, leaving everything else where it is.

## Details

Drag an end out and the recording's picture and sound past the cut come back, everything after the
clip moving later to make room; this is how you bring back a bit that Remove silences took out.
Drag it in and everything after moves up, leaving no gap. The box above the edge says "The rest
follows" while it does.

Hold `Ctrl` to **trim only**: the edge stops at the next clip, and a shorter clip leaves a gap.
Clips on other tracks, a picture on V2 or music on A2, always trim only, so they stay on the
moment they were placed against.

Where the rest cannot follow without something going out of step, such as a voice over left whole
across the cut, the drag trims only there instead.

A clip cannot be trimmed past the footage it has. For trimming in general, see Trim a clip.
