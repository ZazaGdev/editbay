Play a clip faster, as a time-lapse, or slower, as slow motion.

## Steps

1. Select the clip on the timeline. {show: clip on:video}
2. In the Inspector's Speed section, pick a speed from the list. {show: clip-speed-choice or:clip on:video}
3. Or type any speed from 0.1x to 16x in the box beside it, and press `Enter`.
4. Pick 1x to play it at its own speed again.

## Details

A clip's length follows its speed: a 10 second clip at 4x lasts 2.5 seconds, and at 0.5x it lasts
20 seconds. On V1 everything after it moves up or along, as a trim does. On another track a faster
clip leaves a gap, and a slower one needs room after it. The clip shows its speed beside its name.

**Sound** plays at its own pitch from 0.5x to 2x. Faster or slower than that it is silent, unless
you tick [Keep the sound at this speed].

**Slow motion** blends one frame into the next so it plays smoothly. Untick
[Smooth slow motion (blend frames)] for plain repeated frames, which keeps every frame sharp.

Zoom keyframes stay on the picture they were put on, so a zoom speeds up or slows down with the
clip. A clip in a group, an image and a text have no speed.
