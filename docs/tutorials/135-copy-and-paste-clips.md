Put the same clips in again somewhere else, with every setting they had.

## Steps

1. Select one or more clips on the timeline. {show: clip on:picture}
2. Press `Ctrl+C` to copy them, or `Ctrl+X` to cut them.
3. Move the playhead to where they should go. {show: playhead-head}
4. Press `Ctrl+V`. Or right click a gap and choose [Paste here]. {show: track "Right click a gap on a track, then choose Paste here"}

## Details

Pasted clips keep every setting they had: their crop, zoom, look, volume, noise cleanup and
transitions. `Ctrl+X` copies them and takes them out leaving a gap, as `Delete` does. A clip's
right click menu has [Copy], [Cut, leaving a gap] and, once something has been copied,
[Paste at the playhead].

A picture and its sound are copied together and stay linked to each other, not to the clips they
came from. Clips copied together keep their spacing.

A text pasted is a text of its own: change its words and the one it was copied from stays as it
was. So a styled title can be set up once and pasted wherever it is needed.

Paste never covers anything. A clip goes on the track it came from when that stretch is free, and
otherwise on the next free track, with a new one added when none is. Copy in one sequence and
paste in another of the same project works too. Each paste is one undo step.
