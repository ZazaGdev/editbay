Take out a stretch of time across several clips, rather than one clip.

## Steps

1. Put the playhead at the start of the stretch and press `Shift+I`. {show: playhead-head}
2. Put it at the end and press `Shift+O`. The range is drawn across the timeline. {show: ruler}
3. Press `Delete` to leave a gap, or `Shift+Delete` to close it up.

## Details

Sometimes what you want to remove is not a clip, it is a stretch of time across several. With a
range marked, `Delete` and `Shift+Delete` work on **the range** rather than on a selected clip,
cutting through whatever happens to be under it.

This is the fastest way to take out a whole passage: a false start, a long interruption, a section
you have decided against.

The marks stay until you clear or replace them, so you can set a range, look through it, and then
decide. A range can also be exported on its own: [Part] in the Export dialog.
