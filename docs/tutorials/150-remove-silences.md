Find every pause in the talking and take them out, seeing what you get back before anything is cut.

## Steps

1. Press [Remove silences] above the timeline. {show: remove-silences}
2. Pick how strictly a pause is judged: [Careful], [Normal] or [Tight]. {show: remove-silences "Press Remove silences, then pick Careful, Normal or Tight"}
3. Go through the pauses, keeping or taking each one. {show: remove-silences "Press Remove silences, then go through the pauses it lists"}
4. Press the button that takes them out. It says how many, such as Take 12 pauses out. {show: remove-silences "Press Remove silences, then press the button that takes them out"}

## Details

The biggest time saver in the app for anything with talking in it.

It listens to a track you choose, finds every pause, and **shows you what you would get back
before anything is cut.** You see the total time saved, and every pause listed one by one.

Rather than one of the three judgements, you can open the numbers and set them yourself: how quiet
counts as silence, the shortest pause worth taking, and how much to leave either side so a cut does
not clip the start of a word.

In the list, keep or take each pause, or press [Keep all] or [Take all]. `Up`, `Down` and `Enter`
walk it without the mouse.

Nothing is removed until you press that last button, so there is no risk in looking.
