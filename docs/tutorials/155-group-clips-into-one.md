Make a row of pieces act as one clip, so a zoom or framing runs across the cuts.

## Steps

1. Click the first clip on the track. {show: clip on:picture}
2. `Shift`+click the rest beside it on the same track.
3. Press `Ctrl+G`. Or right click one and choose [Join], then [Group into one clip, keeping the cuts]. {show: clip on:picture "Right click a selected clip, then choose Join, then Group into one clip, keeping the cuts"}

## Details

After Remove silences, a talk is a row of short pieces. Each is its own clip, so a zoom or a
framing on one stops at its cut. A group keeps its cuts: nothing comes back, and the pieces play
exactly as they did. Their sound comes with them, and the pieces can come from different
recordings.

A group works as one clip:

- **One click selects it all**, and dragging it moves it all. `Delete` takes it all.
- **It trims at its two ends only.** The cuts inside are marked with a dashed line and have no
  handles.
- **Its framing and zoom are one.** Crop it, place it or give it a Simple zoom and it spans the
  whole group, so a slow zoom runs from the first piece's start to the last piece's end with no
  jump at any cut. Trim the group and the zoom stretches to fit. The export plays the same.
- A look, a colour mark or a layout goes on every piece.

To take it apart, see Ungroup a group. Not the same as Join (`Ctrl+J`), which brings back whatever
was cut between pieces: see Join the pieces of a split clip.
