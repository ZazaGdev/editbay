Take a group of clips apart into its pieces again.

## Steps

1. Select the group on the timeline. {show: clip on:group}
2. Press `Ctrl+Shift+G`. Or right click it and choose [Join], then [Ungroup into its pieces]. {show: clip on:group "Right click the group, then choose Join, then Ungroup into its pieces"}

## Details

Each piece keeps what it shows, zoom and all, so ungrouping changes nothing you see or hear. The
pieces are separate clips again, each with its own trim handles, and a zoom or framing put on one
of them from now on stops at its cut.

To make them one again, see Group clips into one, keeping the cuts.
