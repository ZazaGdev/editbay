Flag the moments worth keeping as they play past, then jump straight back to them.

## Steps

1. Press `M` to drop a marker at the playhead. It works while playing. {show: playhead-head}
2. Press `Shift+Up` and `Shift+Down` to jump to the previous and next marker.
3. Right click a marker's flag to name it, colour it or remove it. {show: marker on:marker "Right click this flag to name, colour or remove it"}

## Details

The fastest way to find the four moments worth keeping in a half hour recording. That `M` works
while playing is the point of it: mark the moment as it goes past, rather than hunting for it
afterwards.

`Shift+Up` and `Shift+Down` jump between markers the same way `Up` and `Down` jump between cuts.

The flags sit on the time bar. Double clicking one does the same as right clicking it. Pressing a
flag takes the playhead to it, and a flag can be dragged along the bar if you dropped it slightly
late.

Markers sit on the sequence rather than on a clip, so one stays put when a clip under it moves.
They are saved with the project and they undo like anything else.
