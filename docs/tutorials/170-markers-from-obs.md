Mark moments in OBS while you record and have them waiting as markers in Editbay.

## Steps

1. In OBS, turn on the Hybrid MP4 recording format.
2. Give OBS's chapter hotkey a key, and press it while recording to mark a moment.
3. In Sources, press the three dots on that recording. {show: asset-more on:asset}
4. Choose the item that brings in its markers. It says how many, such as Bring in its 5 markers. {show: asset-more on:asset "Press the three dots on the recording, then choose Bring in its markers"}

## Details

Every press of the chapter hotkey while you are recording writes a chapter into the file. Bringing
them in turns those chapters into markers, so the moments you flagged live are already marked
before you have cut anything.

It only works with Hybrid MP4, because that is the only format OBS writes chapters into. A
recording with no chapters simply will not offer the option.
