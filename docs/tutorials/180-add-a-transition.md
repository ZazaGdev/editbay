Blend one clip into the next at a cut, or fade a clip in or out.

## Steps

1. Move the pointer near a cut, and press the small button that appears just off the seam. {show: clip on:cut "Point at the cut at this clip's start, then press the small button just off it"}
2. Or right click a clip and choose [Add a transition], then [At its start] or [At its end]. {show: clip on:picture "Right click this clip, then choose Add a transition"}
3. Or press `Ctrl+D` for the nearest edge, or `Ctrl+Shift+D` for the other one.
4. Or press `Ctrl+K` and type transition, like any other action. {show: open-search}

## Details

All four ways do the same thing.

At a clip's edge with nothing beside it, such as the very start of the sequence, the end of the
last clip or a clip after a gap, the button sits just inside the clip and makes a fade in or out.
That is how you fade in from nothing at the start of a video.

The button deliberately never sits on the seam, because that is where the trim handles are. On
clips too narrow to spare the room it stays away entirely, since a button that ate your trims
would be worse than no button.

A new transition is one second long and a Dissolve unless you have changed those. The length and
the kind you last used are remembered, so the next one is the one you left, whichever of the four
ways you add it: pick a push up once, and the button on the next cut adds a push up.

To change one once it is there, see Shape and change a transition.
