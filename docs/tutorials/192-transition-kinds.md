Every kind of transition there is, and how to pick one.

## Steps

1. Right click a transition on the timeline. {show: clip-transition on:transition}
2. Point at a kind in the menu to see it playing, small, beside its name. {show: clip-transition on:transition "Right click the transition, then point at a kind"}
3. For Wipe, Push, Cover or Reveal, point at the family, then choose Left, Right, Up or Down.
4. To change the kind new transitions get, open [Settings], then [Transitions]. {show: open-settings "Open Settings, then Transitions"}

## Details

- **Dissolve**, **Dip to black** and **Dip to white**.
- **Flash**: stays on the picture, goes white just before the cut, and fades back from white.
- **Wipe**, **Push**, **Cover** and **Reveal**, each in four directions. A push moves both
  pictures together; a cover slides the next one in over the one still in place; a reveal slides
  the one going off the next, which stays still behind it.
- **Circle open** and **Circle close**: the next picture opens out of the middle, or closes in
  from the edges, with a soft edge.
- **Iris** and **Box**: the picture closes to black in a circle or a box, and opens on the next.
- **Zoom in**: into the middle of the picture going, then the next fades in.
- **Zoom out**: the next picture fades in enlarged and pulls back to its own size.
- **Blur**: both pictures blurred sideways through the cut.
- **Pixelate**: both pictures break into squares, largest at the cut, while one turns into the
  other.
- **RGB split**: red and blue pull apart through the cut, a glitch look, while one turns into
  the other.
- **Spin**: both pictures turn one whole turn while one turns into the other. The corners show
  black as it turns.

Changing kind keeps the length and position you set. Settings lists the kinds by the same families.
