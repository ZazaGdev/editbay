On a clip with more than one picture, say which one the crop and zoom act on.

## Steps

1. Select a clip with more than one picture, such as a Split. {show: clip on:video}
2. Press [Main], [Picture 2] and so on in [Working on], at the top of the Inspector. {show: picture-switch or:layout on:video "Press Split or Picture in picture here first, so the clip has a second picture"}
3. Or double click the picture on the Output view. {show: monitor-view on:video "Double click a picture on the Output view"}

## Details

A clip with more than one picture is a Split, a picture in picture, or a screen recording with
the camera as Picture 2. The pressed button is the picture [Zoom] and [Crop] act on: the crop
numbers, the view buttons, [Reset crop], [Copy crop] and [Paste crop], and the zoom and its
keyframes. [Working on] stays at the top of the Inspector as you scroll, so you choose once and
work down. The [Pictures] row of [Frame] is the same choice. A clip with one picture has no
[Working on] row.

On the Output view, a double click on a picture chooses it, even a small one sitting under a
full frame picture whose box covers it, and brings its crop into view. A [Crop] group you folded
stays folded.

Each picture has its own zoom too. The [Zoom] group is the zoom of the picture chosen here, and
says which. Choose Picture 2 and zoom it, and the main picture stays as it is; zoom both and each
moves in its own way. On the timeline, the selected clip's keyframe diamonds are the chosen
picture's.

On a picture in picture, a zoom keyframe also holds where the small box is, so the same diamonds
can move the box out of the way and back. See Put one recording over another.
