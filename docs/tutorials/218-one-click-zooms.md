Make the common zooms in one click, aimed at whatever you click on.

## Steps

1. Select a clip and put the playhead where the zoom should happen, or mark In and Out. {show: clip on:video}
2. In [Zoom], with [Keyframes] chosen, find the One-click zooms part and press [Punch in], [Zoom in] or [Zoom in and out]. {show: zoom-punch or:zoom-mode on:video "Choose Keyframes here, then press Punch in"}
3. Click the face or line you want on the Source or Output view. {show: monitor-view on:video "Click where it should close in"}
4. Press `Enter`, or the button again, when it is right.

## Details

[Punch in] jumps closer on the frame at the playhead, which hides a jump cut. [Zoom in] eases in
from the playhead. [Zoom in and out] eases in, holds and eases back out, over three seconds
from the playhead. With a range marked, they work across In to Out.

[How far] sets how close they go, 150% to begin with. [How quick] sets how long Zoom in and Zoom
in and out take to get there: [Quick] is half a second, [Normal] a second, [Slow] two seconds.
Both are remembered.

They close in on the middle of the picture. Click on the Source or Output view straight after
pressing the button and the zoom moves there instead, a small ring marks the spot, and it is
still one step to undo.

What they make are ordinary keyframes, so you can move or ease them as in Zoom with keyframes. A
Punch in is a [Jump] on purpose: to make it glide instead, put the playhead just before it and
choose [Smooth] in [To the next keyframe].
To keep a moving face in the crop, see Keep a face in frame.
