See a clip's zoom keyframes on the timeline, to line them up with the sound.

## Steps

1. Look along the bottom of a clip that zooms for its diamonds. {show: clip-keyframe on:keyframes "The diamonds along the bottom of a clip that zooms"}
2. Click a diamond to go to it. {show: clip-keyframe on:keyframes}
3. Drag a diamond left or right to move that keyframe. The pointer turns into a two-way arrow over it. {show: clip-keyframe on:keyframes}
4. To hide them all, choose [Show zoom keyframes on clips] in `Ctrl+K`. {show: open-search "Press Ctrl+K, then choose Show zoom keyframes on clips"}

## Details

The diamonds are faint on every clip that zooms and bright on the one selected, so you can line
them up with the sound and see where a split would land.

While you drag, the time it will land on shows above the diamond. It snaps to the playhead and
to the edges of clips when [Snap] is on, unless you hold `Alt`, and it stays on its own clip: the
clip itself never moves. One `Ctrl+Z` puts it back.

Keyframes too close together at the timeline's zoom are one larger diamond until you zoom in, and
that one only goes there when clicked. A Simple zoom's two diamonds, at the clip's two ends, also
only go there.

On a clip with more than one picture, the selected clip's diamonds are the chosen picture's. See
Choose which picture you are framing.

To add, remove or ease keyframes, see Zoom with keyframes.
