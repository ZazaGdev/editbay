Choose where a picture sits in the finished frame.

## Steps

1. Select the clip on the timeline. {show: clip on:video}
2. Switch the monitor to [Output], or press `V`. {show: view}
3. Drag the picture where you want it. {show: monitor-picture or:monitor-view on:video "Drag the picture on the Output view"}
4. Or press [Full frame] or [Corner] in the inspector. {show: picture-full on:overlay}

## Details

Cropping says what you are using. Placing says where it sits in the finished frame.

[Full frame] puts the picture back over everything, and [Corner] snaps it to a box in the top
right.

The two together are the whole framing model. There is nothing else to learn: a box over the
source, and a position in the output.

On a vertical sequence there is room to show both views at once, side by side, with [Both]. `V`
then chooses which one your keys act on rather than swapping what you can see.

The picture lines up with the middle, the edges and other pictures as you drag. See Line pictures
up as you drag.
