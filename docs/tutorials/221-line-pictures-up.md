Pictures snap to the middle, the edges and each other as you move them on the Output view.

## Steps

1. Drag a picture on the Output view. A thin line shows what it lines up with. {show: monitor-picture or:monitor-view on:video "Drag a picture on the Output view"}
2. Hold `Alt` while you drag to place it freely, with no snapping.
3. Press [Snap] under the monitor to turn snapping off for good, or on again. {show: place-snap}

## Details

Near the middle of the frame, an edge, a third, the title safe area or the edge or middle of
anything else on screen, the box snaps to it and a thin line across the frame shows what it is
lined up with. Resizing does the same for the side or corner you drag. The line goes when you
move on or let go. While you hold `Alt`, a note under the box says snapping is off.

[Snap] sits beside [Guides] under the monitor. Off, a picture goes exactly where you put it, and
`Alt` snaps it for that one drag instead. It is kept, and it is apart from the timeline's own
Snap.

The title safe area is the safe zone chosen under [Guides] on a vertical sequence, and a tenth in
from each side otherwise. See Guides and safe zones.
