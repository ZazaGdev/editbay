Change a clip's colour with a ready-made look, then fine tune it.

## Steps

1. Select a clip, then open [Look] in the inspector. {show: clip on:picture "Select this clip, then open Look in the inspector"}
2. Pick a look from the [Colour] dropdown. {show: look-select on:picture}
3. Set how much of it you get with [Strength].
4. Fine tune with [Brightness], [Contrast], [Saturation] and [Warmth].

## Details

There are seven looks: [Black and white], [Sepia], [Warm], [Cool], [Faded], [High contrast] and
[Vivid]. [None] gives the recording its own colour back.

Once a clip has a look, [Strength] sets how much of it you get. At 50%, Black and white is half
way to grey.

The four sliders each run from -100% to 100%, and 0 is the recording as it was. They act on top
of the look, so Black and white can be made brighter without any colour coming back.
[Reset the sliders] puts all four back to 0.

A look is on every picture of the clip, and on every piece of a group. The Output view shows
exactly what the export writes, so what you see is what you get.
