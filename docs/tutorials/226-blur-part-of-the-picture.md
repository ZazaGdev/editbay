Hide a name, an email or a notification in a screen recording under a blurred box.

## Steps

1. Select the clip, then open [Blur] in the inspector. {show: clip on:video "Select this clip, then open Blur in the inspector"}
2. Press [Add a blurred box]. {show: blur-box-add on:video}
3. Drag the box over what you want hidden on the Source view, and drag its corners or sides to size it. {show: monitor-view on:video "Drag the blurred box on the Source view"}
4. Set how much it is blurred with its slider, then press its [Box 1] button again when you are done.

## Details

A new box goes in the middle of the recording and shows on the Source view. Press its Box button
later to move it again. A clip can have up to eight boxes, and the small cross takes one away.

A box sits on the recording itself, so it stays over the same thing however the clip is cropped
or zoomed. It lasts the whole clip: to hide something for only part of it, split the clip first.

[Whole picture] blurs all of it, for a background behind text, say.

The Output view shows the blur exactly as the export writes it.
