Set the framing once and give it to the other clips cut from the same recording.

## Steps

1. Select the clip that is framed right and press `Ctrl+Alt+C`. {show: clip on:video}
2. Select another clip and press `Ctrl+Alt+V`.
3. Or right click a clip and choose [Framing], then [Copy the framing] or [Paste the framing]. {show: clip on:video "Right click this clip, then choose Framing"}
4. For every clip of this recording on the track, press [Apply to all clips of this recording] in [Frame]. {show: apply-layout-all on:video}

## Details

When you cut one recording into several clips, they all usually want the same framing. The crop,
the placement and the layout all come across together.

[Apply to all clips of this recording] gives a clip's framing to every other clip from the same
recording on the same track. A camera or a screen capture usually does not move for a whole
session, so one layout genuinely does suit every clip cut out of it.

Framing is not sound, so pasting it never touches a clip's volume.

The [Framing] menu also holds your saved layouts. To take the crop alone, see Copy just the
crop.
