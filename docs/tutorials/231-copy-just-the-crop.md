Give other clips the same crop, while each keeps its own placement and layout.

## Steps

1. Select the clip with the crop you want and press [Copy crop] in [Crop], or `Ctrl+Alt+Shift+C`. {show: crop-copy on:video}
2. Select one or more other clips. {show: clip on:video}
3. Press [Paste crop], or `Ctrl+Alt+Shift+V`. {show: crop-paste on:video}

## Details

Only the crop changes: each clip keeps its own placement, layers and layout, and one undo puts
them all back.

The crop goes onto the picture chosen at the top of [Crop]: with Picture 2 chosen, each clip's
Picture 2 gets it and its main picture keeps its own. Selecting another clip keeps Picture 2
chosen, and a clip with no Picture 2 is left alone.

A clip from a recording of another size gets the same part of the picture, not the same pixels.

Both are in a clip's right click menu too, under [Framing], as [Copy the crop] and
[Paste the crop]. The pastes show there once something has been copied.
