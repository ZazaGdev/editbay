See where TikTok, Reels and Shorts put their buttons over your video, and the thirds to compose by.

## Steps

1. Press `G` to draw the guides over the Output view, or press [Guides] under the monitor for the choices. {show: guides-toggle}
2. Under the lines to compose by, tick [Centre and thirds]. {show: guides-toggle "Press Guides, then tick Centre and thirds"}
3. Under where the apps put their buttons, choose [TikTok], [Reels], [Shorts] or [All three]. {show: guides-toggle "Press Guides, then choose an app"}

## Details

Each of those apps puts its own buttons, captions and username over your video, and the safe
zones show you where. Keeping faces and text clear of those areas is the difference between a
caption being readable and being hidden behind a follow button. The safe zones are for a frame
taller than it is wide; on a landscape or square sequence there is nothing to draw.

The guides are drawn over the Output view only in the app. They never appear in what you export.

`F` puts the monitor full screen when you want to judge a frame properly, and `Shift+F` fits the
whole edit on the timeline instead.
