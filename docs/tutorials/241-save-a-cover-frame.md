Pick a cover for Instagram and save the frame at the playhead as a PNG.

## Steps

1. Press [Guides] under the monitor and tick [Instagram grid, 3:4]. {show: guides-toggle "Press Guides, then tick Instagram grid, 3:4"}
2. Move the playhead to the frame you want, with what matters inside the outline.
3. Press `Ctrl+Alt+S` to save the frame as a PNG.

## Details

[Instagram grid, 3:4] outlines the part of a vertical frame your profile grid shows of a cover.

`Ctrl+Alt+S` saves the frame at the playhead as a PNG in your export folder. It works from
anywhere in the app, and is in `Ctrl+K` too.

The outline, like all the guides, is drawn only in the app and never appears in what you export.
