Zoom either view of the monitor in for a closer look, without changing the clip or the project.

## Steps

1. Hold `Ctrl` and turn the mouse wheel over the Source or Output view. {show: monitor-view}
2. To look around while zoomed, drag with the middle button, or hold `Space` and drag.
3. Press [Reset] at the top right of the view to fit the whole picture again.

## Details

The zoom goes around the pointer, so what you are pointing at stays under it. On the Source view
the wheel alone zooms too. The top right of a zoomed view says how far in it is, such as 200%.

Dragging with `Space` held uses the left button. Tapping `Space` without a drag still plays and
pauses.

Each view keeps its own zoom. The Output view goes up to 400%, the Source view to 800%. Boxes you
drag on a zoomed view, such as the crop or a text, move as they would fitted, only in finer steps.

None of this is an edit, so it never appears in your undo history, and the export is unchanged.
