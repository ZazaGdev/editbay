Change a sequence's shape and its framing in one step, from a layout you saved.

## Steps

1. Select a clip framed the way you want. {show: clip on:video}
2. Open the shape list beside the sequence tabs and choose [Save current as...]. {show: export-preset "Open this list, then choose Save current as..."}
3. Type a name and press `Enter` to keep it.
4. Later, pick the saved layout from the same list. {show: export-preset "Open this list, then pick your saved layout"}

## Details

Your saved layouts are listed under the four shapes, each with the shape it was saved in.
[Save current as...] saves the selected clip's framing, in this shape, under the name you type.

Picking a saved layout changes the sequence to that shape and gives every recording on it that
layout's framing, in one step you can undo. Pictures, texts and black screens keep their own.
