Put words over your video, and type them straight onto the picture.

## Steps

1. Press `T`, or the text button at the left of the timeline's header bar. {show: add-text}
2. Double click the text on the Output view and type the words. {show: monitor-picture or:monitor-view on:text "Double click the text on the Output view"}
3. Press `Ctrl+Enter`, or click anywhere else, to finish.
4. Drag the text to move it, a corner to resize it, and the round handle to turn it. {show: monitor-picture or:monitor-view on:text "Drag the text on the Output view"}

## Details

The text goes on at the playhead, on the first track above V1 with room. It is five seconds
long and stretches like any clip. A new text starts as the last one you changed looks, saying
"Your text".

**Typing on the Output view.** It types where the text sits. `Enter` starts a new line, and
`Escape` puts the old words back. While you type, keys like `T` and `Space` type letters rather
than working the editor. The letters stay at the text's size from the first one you type; only
the box around them grows and shrinks. The whole of the typing is one undo. You can also type
the words in the Inspector's [Text] section, where several lines are fine and the preview
follows as you type.

Moving, resizing and turning are each one undo. [Opacity] is in the Frame section. To have it pop or fade in, see Make it pop or fade in. For its
font, colours and the rest, see Style a text.

**Deleting a text** with `Delete` leaves the playhead where it is, with nothing selected, so you
carry on from the same moment.

A project opened on a PC without the font you chose draws the text in a stand-in font.
