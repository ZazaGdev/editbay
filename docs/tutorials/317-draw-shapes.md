Point at something in a screen recording by drawing a shape over it.

## Steps

1. Press the shapes button at the left of the timeline's header bar, next to the text button. {show: add-shape}
2. Pick a shape, such as [Arrow], [Box] or [Circle]. {show: add-shape "Press this, then pick a shape"}
3. Drag on the Output view: for an arrow, from where it starts to where it points. {show: monitor-stage}
4. Trim its clip like any clip. {show: clip on:shape}

## Details

The menu lists [Arrow], [Curved arrow], [Line], [Box], [Rounded box], [Circle], [Highlight],
[Pen] and [Highlighter], and any styles of your own. For a box or a circle, drag from one corner
to the other. A click without a drag makes one of a usable size there. `Escape` stops without
drawing. A [Highlight] is a see-through yellow box with no outline, for marking a line of code or
a button.

The shape lands as a clip of its own on the first track above V1, at the playhead, five seconds
long, and is selected.

**Arrows and lines** have a handle on each end and one in the middle. Drag an end to point it
somewhere else, and the middle one to bend it into a curve; drag the middle back onto the line
to straighten it. [Bend] and [Straighten] in the Inspector do the same.

Like a text, a shape moves, grows and turns on the Output view. Its outline keeps its width when
you make it larger. The preview and the export show exactly the same picture.

For drawing by hand, see Draw by hand with the pen. For its colours, see Change a shape's look.
