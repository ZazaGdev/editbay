One folder for the sounds, overlays and clips you use again, listed in Sources as Assets.

## Steps

1. In Sources, press [Choose folder] beside No assets folder yet. {show: assets-choose or:source-folders "Your assets folder is already chosen: it is here in Sources"}
2. Or open [Settings], then [Sources], then [Assets]. {show: open-settings "Open Settings, then Sources, then Assets"}
3. Put files in its Video, Audio and Images folders. They appear in Sources. {show: source-folders}

## Details

Keep the video, sound and pictures you use again, such as sound effects, overlays and green
screen clips, in one assets folder, and Editbay lists it in Sources as [Assets]. Until you have
one, Sources shows a note with a [Choose folder] button; you can also choose it in [Settings],
under [Sources], in [Assets].

Editbay makes [Video], [Audio] and [Images] folders in it. Files you add to it appear in Sources
the next time it looks.

A folder you chose as your effects folder before is your assets folder now, where it was.
