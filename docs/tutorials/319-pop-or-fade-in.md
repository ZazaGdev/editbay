Have an image, a text or a shape pop or fade in, and out again.

## Steps

1. Select the image, text or shape clip on the timeline. {show: clip on:overlay}
2. In the Inspector, under [Opacity], set [Comes in] to [Fade] or [Pop]. {show: appear-in on:overlay}
3. Set [Goes out] the same way.
4. To change how long it takes, type another length in the box beside it.

## Details

[Comes in] and [Goes out] each offer [None], [Fade] or [Pop], in the Frame section. Each starts
at half a second.

A fade brings it in from nothing, or takes it out to nothing. A pop grows it from its middle, a
little past its size, and settles with a little bounce, like a sticker slapped on; going out, it
shrinks away.

The preview and the export show exactly the same thing. Cut the clip in two and the first piece
keeps the way in, the second the way out.
