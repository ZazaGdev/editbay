Lay out a clip with more than one picture in it, in one click.

## Steps

1. Select the clip on the timeline. {show: clip on:video}
2. In the Inspector's Frame section, press [Single], [Split] or [Picture in picture]. {show: layout on:video}
3. Drag either picture on the Output view to adjust it. {show: monitor-picture or:monitor-view on:video "Drag either picture on the Output view"}

## Details

- [Single]: one picture, filling the frame.
- [Split]: two pictures side by side, or stacked, each filling half.
- [Picture in picture]: one large, one small in a corner over it.

Picking a layout arranges the pictures for you. You can still drag either one afterwards, so the
layout is a starting point rather than a cage.

**Each picture has its own colour.** On a clip with more than one picture, Main is blue,
Picture 2 orange, Picture 3 green and so on, the same everywhere: the dot on its button in Frame
and Crop, its box on the Output view and the crop box on the Source view, each with its name on
a tag, and a tag beside the Zoom and Crop headings. Point at a picture on the Output view to see
its name before you choose it.

For the small picture's corner, size and border, see Set up picture in picture. For an OBS
canvas with two views side by side, see Split one recording into two pictures.
