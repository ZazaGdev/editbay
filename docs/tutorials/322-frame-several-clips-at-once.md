Move, resize or crop a picture on many clips in one go.

## Steps

1. Click one clip, then `Shift`+click the rest. {show: clip on:picture}
2. Move or resize a picture on the Output view. {show: monitor-picture or:monitor-view on:picture "Move or resize a picture on the Output view"}
3. Or drag its crop on the Source view, or type its crop numbers.

## Details

Every selected clip that has that picture gets the same place and the same crop, as one step you
can undo. It suits every piece Remove silences left.

Only what you changed follows, the place or the crop, nothing else. The Inspector says how many
clips a change reaches, and how many are left alone for not having that picture. A crop number
the selected clips disagree on reads **Mixed** until you type one.
