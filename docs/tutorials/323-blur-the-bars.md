Fill the black bars around a picture with a blurred copy of it.

## Steps

1. Select the clip on the timeline. {show: clip on:video}
2. Under [Bars] in the Inspector, tick [Fill with the picture, blurred]. {show: picture-blur-fill on:video}

## Details

Where a picture does not fill its box, as a landscape recording in a vertical frame, the bars
become the same picture, enlarged and blurred, instead of black.
