Keep a framing you like, such as your game with your facecam in a corner, and give it to any clip in one click.

## Steps

1. Frame a clip the way you like. {show: clip on:video}
2. Under [Saved layouts] in the Frame group, press [Save this framing] and give it a name. {show: save-layout on:video}
3. Later, select any picture clip, in any project, and click the layout's name.

## Details

With several clips selected, they all get it, in one step you can undo. Saving under a name you
already have updates that layout.

Right click a layout to update it with the selected clip's framing, rename it, or delete it (the
note that says so has an [Undo]).

A layout holds the pictures, their crops and places, and the layout, as Copy framing does; the
zoom and the look are left out.

A layout also appears in the shape list by the sequence tabs, where one pick sets the shape and
gives every recording in the sequence its framing.
