A recording you add to a new project comes framed the way you framed the last one.

## Steps

1. Put a recording on a project where nothing is framed yet. {show: timeline-tracks}
2. If it is the same size as the one you framed last, it is framed at once. Press [Undo] on the note to have it as it came.
3. If it is another size, a note asks Use your last framing? Press [Use], or [Dismiss].

## Details

Editbay remembers how you last framed a recording: its crop, where it sits, its layout and any
further pictures.

- **The same size** as the recording you framed last, in width and height: it is framed that way
  at once, and a note says so, with [Undo].
- **Another size**: [Use] gives your last framing, scaled to this recording. [Dismiss], or doing
  nothing, leaves the recording as it came.

It keeps going for every recording you add, an episode recorded as three files included, until
you frame one yourself. A project with a recording you framed by hand is never changed.

Images, texts, shapes and colours are left out: each is placed on its own.
