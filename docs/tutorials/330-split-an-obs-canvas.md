Lay out an OBS canvas that holds your game and your face side by side, with no second track.

## Steps

1. Select the clip of that one recording. {show: clip on:video}
2. In the Inspector, press [Split] or [Picture in picture]. {show: layout on:video}
3. Press [Swap] to exchange the two pictures.

## Details

If both pictures are already in **one** recording, you do not need a second track at all.
Editbay cuts the canvas into two pictures and lays them out for you.

It recognises this shape automatically: a recording much wider than it is tall, such as a
3840x1080 canvas made of two 1920x1080 views, is treated as two pictures rather than one very
wide one.

This is usually better than recording two separate files, because the two views are already in
perfect sync and can never drift apart.
