Take a video track out of the preview and the export without deleting anything.

## Steps

1. Press the hide control on the video track's header. {show: track-hide}
2. Press it again to show the track.

## Details

Hiding a track takes it out of the preview **and out of the export**, the same way mute works for
sound.

This is how you check what the edit looks like without an overlay, or export a version with the
facecam removed, without deleting anything or undoing your work afterwards.

Hiding is part of the edit, so it is saved with the project and it undoes. It is not a viewing
convenience, which matters: if you leave a track hidden and export, it really will be missing,
and that is the intended behaviour rather than a trap.
