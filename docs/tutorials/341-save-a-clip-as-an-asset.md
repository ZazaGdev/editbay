Keep a trimmed, edited clip in your assets folder to use again.

## Steps

1. Right click a clip on the timeline. {show: clip on:picture}
2. Choose [Save as asset]. {show: clip on:picture "Right click this clip, then choose Save as asset"}
3. For its sound alone, `Alt`+click the sound first, then right click it.

## Details

The clip is saved as it plays, trimmed and with its edits, into the assets folder:

- a picture with its sound, when its sound is selected too, goes into [Video]
- a sound clip alone (`Alt`+click it) goes into [Audio]
- a still picture goes into [Images]

A note says where it went. You need an assets folder first: see Your assets folder.
