Put a sound effect or overlay from your assets exactly where you want it.

## Steps

1. Open your [Assets] in Sources and find the effect. {show: source-folders}
2. Drag it onto a track. {show: timeline-row}
3. Let go where it should start.

## Details

The effect lands where you let go, on the track you let go over. It is added to the Source bin
as it lands.

To line an effect up with a clip without dragging, see Put an effect over a clip.
