Line an effect up with the start of a clip in two clicks.

## Steps

1. Select the clip on the timeline. {show: clip on:picture}
2. In your assets, right click the effect, or press its [...] button. {show: effect-more or:source-folder on:effect "Open your assets folder here, then right click the effect"}
3. Choose [Put over the selected clip]. {show: effect-more or:source-folder on:effect "Open your assets folder here, then press ... on the effect and choose Put over the selected clip"}

## Details

A recording or image goes on the video track above the clip, starting where the clip starts. A
sound goes on a free audio track from the same point.

The clip itself is left as it was, and stays selected, so you can put a second effect over it
straight away.
