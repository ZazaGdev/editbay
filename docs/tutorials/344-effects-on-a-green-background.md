An effect on a green, blue or black background has the background taken out for you.

## Steps

1. Put the effect on the timeline as usual. The background goes on its own. {show: effect-more or:source-folder on:effect "Open your assets folder here, then drag the effect onto the timeline"}
2. If it stays, choose [Put over it, removing the background] and pick the colour. {show: effect-more or:source-folder on:effect "Open your assets folder here, then press ... on the effect and choose Put over it, removing the background"}
3. To change it later, open the clip's [Tools]. {show: clip on:picture "Right click this clip, then choose Tools"}

## Details

When an effect lands, Editbay looks for a green, blue or black background and takes it out, so
only the effect shows over your footage.

If it does not see the background for itself, use [Put over it, removing the background] in the
effect's menu and pick the colour. You can change it later from the clip's [Tools]: see Remove a
background.
