Free packs you may use in published, monetised videos.

## Steps

1. Under your folders in Sources, press [Get free assets]. {show: free-packs-open}
2. Press [Open page] on a pack and download it in your browser.
3. Unpack it into your assets folder.
4. If the pack needs a credit, put its line in your video's description.

## Details

[Get free assets] shows once you have an assets folder. It lists free packs whose licences allow
them in published, monetised videos: what is in each, how it comes, and whether a video using it
needs a credit in its description, with the exact line.

[Open page] opens the pack in your browser; download it and unpack it into your assets folder
yourself. For a pack that needs a credit, keep that line in a `credit.txt` in the pack's folder,
and every export lists it for you.
