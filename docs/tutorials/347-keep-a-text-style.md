Save a text's look to use again, or give every text the same look at once.

## Steps

1. Select a text you like the look of. {show: clip on:text}
2. Press [Save as my style] near the bottom of the Text section, and name it. {show: text-saved on:text}
3. On another text, press that style's button to give it the look.
4. Or press [Apply to all texts] to give every text this look. {show: text-apply-all or:text-panel on:text "Apply to all texts shows in the Text section once there is another text"}

## Details

**Save as my style** keeps the text's whole look under a name, without its words. Your text
styles show as buttons above it in every project: press one to give the selected text that look,
keeping its words and alignment, in one undo. The x beside a style forgets it. Text styles are
kept apart from caption styles. [Settings], under [Styles], renames or forgets them too.

**Apply to all texts**, under [Reset to default], gives every other text in the project, in every
sequence, this text's font, size, colours, outline, shadow and box, once you say you are sure.
Each keeps its own words, alignment, place on the frame and timing; a bigger size grows its box
from the side its alignment keeps still. One undo puts them all back.
