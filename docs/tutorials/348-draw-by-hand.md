Draw a stroke by hand over the video: round a button, under a line of code, a quick tick.

## Steps

1. Press the shapes button at the left of the timeline's header bar. {show: add-shape}
2. Pick [Pen] or [Highlighter]. {show: add-shape "Press this, then pick Pen or Highlighter"}
3. Press and drag on the Output view along the path you want. {show: monitor-stage}
4. Let go to finish the stroke.

## Details

When you let go, the stroke becomes a shape like any other: a clip of its own on the first track
above V1, at the playhead, five seconds long.

The [Highlighter] is a wide, see-through yellow stroke. A stroke is smoothed a little as it is
kept, so a shaky hand still draws a clean line.

To have the stroke draw itself on, see Make a shape draw itself on.
