Have a shape's outline draw itself, as if drawn live.

## Steps

1. Select the shape's clip on the timeline. {show: clip on:shape}
2. In the Inspector's [Shape] section, under [Draw on], choose [Quick], [Steady] or [Slow]. {show: shape-draw-on on:shape}
3. Play it from the start of the clip to see it.

## Details

The outline draws itself from where it starts, over half a second for [Quick], one second for
[Steady] or two seconds for [Slow], from the moment the clip begins; then it stays. It suits an
arrow pointing at something, or a circle drawn round it.

A box's fill and an arrow's head come in as the outline finishes. [Off] shows it whole from the
start.

To have the whole shape pop or fade in instead, see Make it pop or fade in.
