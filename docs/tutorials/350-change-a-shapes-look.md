Set a shape's colours, outline, fill and shadow, and keep a look you like.

## Steps

1. Select the shape's clip on the timeline. {show: clip on:shape}
2. In the Inspector's [Shape] section, set its [Outline], [Fill] and [Shadow]. {show: shape-stroke on:shape}
3. To keep the look, press [Save as my style] and name it.
4. Next time, pick it in the shapes menu under Your styles, and drag. {show: add-shape}

## Details

The [Shape] section sets the outline's colour and width, a fill and how see-through it is,
rounded corners for a box, arrowheads at either end of a line, and a soft shadow so it reads over
busy footage. Its [Opacity], [Comes in] and [Goes out] (fade or pop) are in the Frame section.

**Keep a look you like.** [Save as my style] at the bottom of the Shape section names it and
puts it in the shapes menu, under Your styles, in every project. Pick it and drag, and the new
shape looks the same. The small cross beside a saved style removes it, and [Settings], under
[Styles], renames or forgets them too.
