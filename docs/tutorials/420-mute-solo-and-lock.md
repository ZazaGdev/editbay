Silence a track, hear one track alone, or protect a finished track from edits.

## Steps

1. Press [M] on a track header to mute it, in the edit and the export. {show: track-mute}
2. Press [S] to hear that track alone while you listen. {show: track-solo}
3. Press the lock to stop the track being edited. {show: track-lock}

## Details

Every track header carries these controls, and a sound track's has one more, [D], which ducks it
under the others: see Duck music under a voice.

Mute is part of the edit: it is saved, it undoes, and a muted track is silent in what you export.
That is what you want for a track you have decided against.

Solo is only for listening. It is never saved and never affects an export. It exists so you can
hear one track on its own for a moment without changing anything.

The lock stops a track being edited at all. Useful once a track is finished and you do not want a
stray drag to move it while you work on the ones above.

The distinction between mute and solo is the one worth remembering: one is a decision, the other
is a pair of headphones.
