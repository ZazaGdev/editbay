Find a moment by ear: drag the playhead and hear the sound under it.

## Steps

1. Drag the playhead along the time bar. {show: playhead-head}
2. Stop when you hear the word or laugh you are looking for.

## Details

You hear the sound under the playhead as you go, in short bursts.

This is usually much faster than reading a waveform for a moment you only half remember. You
recognise a word or a laugh as you pass it, and stop there.

Only the clip actually under the playhead is heard, which matters on a busy timeline: without
that you would hear the start of the next clip playing over the one you are listening for.

It follows the preview volume, so if you have turned the app down, scrubbing is quiet too.
