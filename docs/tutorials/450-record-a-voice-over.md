Record from your microphone straight onto an audio track.

## Steps

1. Put the playhead where the voice over should start. {show: playhead-head}
2. Press `R`, or the record button. {show: record}
3. Press it again to stop and keep the take. {show: record}

## Details

Takes are saved as files in a folder beside the project file, so they travel with the project. If
you move the project to another drive, the voice overs go with it rather than breaking.

Windows asks for microphone permission the first time. If it has been refused, the app says so
in words rather than silently recording nothing: check Settings, Privacy and security,
Microphone, and allow desktop apps.

The recording lands as an ordinary clip on an ordinary audio track, so everything else in this
section applies to it: volume, mute, trimming, moving.
