A recording's sound stays linked to its picture, and you can unlink it when you need to.

## Steps

1. Add a recording: its sound arrives on audio tracks, linked to the picture.
2. To move the sound on its own, right click the clip and choose [Unlink]. {show: clip on:linked "Right click this clip, then choose Unlink"}
3. Or press [Unlink] in the inspector's [Linked] group. {show: unlink on:linked}

## Details

Each recording's sound arrives on its own audio tracks, linked to its picture so the two move
together. Split a clip and the sound splits with it. Move it and the sound follows. That is what
stops picture and sound drifting apart while you work.

A recording with several audio tracks, as OBS often produces, brings all of them in. You can then
mix them, mute the ones you do not want, or use one for captions and another for the music.

Unlink only when you genuinely need to move sound independently of its picture, and do it
deliberately: once unlinked, nothing keeps them in step for you any more.
