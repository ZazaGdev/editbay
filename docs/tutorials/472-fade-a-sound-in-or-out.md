Fade a sound up from silence at its start, or down to silence at its end.

## Steps

1. Find the small handle at the top corner of the sound clip's free end. {show: sound-clip on:sound "Point at the top corner of this clip's start or end"}
2. Drag it into the clip, as far as the fade should last.
3. To take the fade away, drag it back to the edge.

## Details

The handle is there when nothing is against that end of the clip. The sound fades up from
silence, or down to silence, over as far as you dragged. The whole drag is one undo.

Once a fade is there it is drawn over the clip like any transition, with handles at its ends to
make it longer or shorter, and a right click for its kinds: [Even], [Linear] and [Smooth].

Where two sounds meet, there is no fade handle. Point at the top of the cut instead and use its
button, which makes a crossfade between them: see Crossfade between two sounds.

A fade on a sound never touches the picture it came with: the picture carries on at full
strength while its sound fades.
