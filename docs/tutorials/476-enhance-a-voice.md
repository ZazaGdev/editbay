Make a voice clearer and more even, without changing how loud it is.

## Steps

1. Select the sound clip on the timeline. {show: sound-clip on:sound}
2. In the inspector's [Sound] group, tick [Enhance voice]. {show: sound-clip-voice on:sound}
3. Choose [Light], [Normal] or [Strong] beside it.
4. On a noisy recording, tick [Clean up noise] too.

## Details

The voice comes out clearer and more even, in the preview and in the export alike: the rumble
under it cut, the boxy middle taken down, the part that makes words clear lifted, loud and quiet
moments brought closer together, and the hiss of an S softened.

Normal suits most voices. Light keeps a deep or warm voice closer to how it was; Strong sounds
close and even, like radio.

It stays as loud as it was, so the balance against music does not move: only how it sounds
changes. The first time, the app makes an enhanced copy of that recording's track in the
background. Until it is ready the clip plays without it and the inspector says so; an export
started meanwhile waits for it.

Clean up noise matters on a noisy recording because evening out a voice brings the room up with
it, so the noise is taken out first and the voice enhanced after.
