Set up the mic so fewer breaths and mouth clicks reach the recording at all.

## Steps

1. Put the mic to the side of your mouth rather than in front of it.
2. Keep your talking peaks around -12 dB in OBS rather than higher.
3. Keep water to hand while you record.

## Details

Better still than softening breaths and removing clicks afterwards is recording fewer of them.

With the mic to the side of your mouth, breaths blow past it rather than into it. Clicks come
from a dry mouth, so water helps.

Whatever still gets through, the [Breaths] and [Remove mouth clicks] switches in the inspector's
[Sound] group can take down: see Soften breaths and remove mouth clicks.
