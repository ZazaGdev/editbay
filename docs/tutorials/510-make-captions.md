Turn the speech in your sequence into captions, made on this computer.

## Steps

1. Press [Captions] in the top bar. {show: open-captions}
2. Under Listen to, tick the audio tracks with speech on them.
3. Press [Make captions].
4. Press [Run in background] to keep editing while it listens.

## Details

Editbay transcribes the speech **on your own machine**. Nothing is uploaded and there is no API
key.

**Keep editing while it listens.** A long recording takes a few minutes. Close the window, or
press [Run in background], and carry on: the [Captions] button at the top shows how far it has
got, and pressing it opens the window again with the progress and a [Cancel] button. When it
finishes, the captions appear and a message says how many were made, with Undo. Cuts and moves
you make in the meantime are fine: each word still lands on the footage it was heard from.

Pick your tracks carefully. On a recording with game audio on one audio track and a microphone
on another, listening only to the microphone gives far better results than listening to
everything at once.

Captions land on their own track above the picture. From there you can fix the words, retime
them, restyle them and move them.

Captions are burned into the picture when you export, using the font bundled with the app, so
they look the same everywhere and do not depend on a player supporting subtitles. To caption only
part of the sequence, see Captions for part of the sequence.
