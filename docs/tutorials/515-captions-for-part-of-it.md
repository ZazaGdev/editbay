Caption one clip or one stretch, and leave every other caption as it is.

## Steps

1. Select the clips, or mark In and Out with `I` and `O`. {show: clip on:picture}
2. Press [Captions] in the top bar. {show: open-captions}
3. Under Make captions for, choose [The selected clips] or [In to Out].
4. Press [Make captions].

## Details

With clips selected, or In and Out marked, the window offers **Make captions for**: [The whole
sequence], [The selected clips], or [In to Out]. Only that part is listened to, which is
quicker, and only the captions starting in it are replaced; every other caption, words you fixed
by hand included, stays as it was. A note says which captions it replaces.

It is the way to caption a clip added at the end, or to redo one part that came out badly.

A clip's right click menu has [Make captions for this clip] too.
