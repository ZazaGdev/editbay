Fix when a caption shows, and where one line ends and the next begins.

## Steps

1. Drag a caption on the timeline to move it. {show: caption on:caption}
2. Drag its edges to trim it. {show: caption-trim-end on:caption}
3. To break a line, select the caption, put the playhead there and press `S`. {show: caption on:caption}
4. To change how many words each caption holds, set [Words at a time]. {show: caption-style-open on:caption "Press Aa, then set Words at a time under How captions look"}

## Details

A caption behaves like a clip. Drag it to move it, drag its edges to trim it, and split it where
a line should break.

When you nudge a caption's timing it **stops against its neighbours** rather than running over
the words next door, so tightening one does not quietly break the two either side of it.

[Words at a time] is how many words a caption holds wherever the speech allows. A caption can
still hold fewer: one never runs past the end of a sentence or across a real pause, and it
prefers to break at a comma or before "and". At four or five words a caption too wide for the
frame goes onto a second line.

[Group again] breaks every caption again where a reader would pause. Words you fixed stay fixed;
captions you split or trimmed are grouped again with the rest.

For the font, colours and size, see Style captions. To move captions on the frame, see Move
captions on the picture.
