Set the font, size, colours, outline and shadow of every caption in the sequence.

## Steps

1. Press [Aa] on the Captions track to open How captions look. {show: caption-style-open on:caption}
2. Choose a [Font] and set [Size] and [Height in frame]. {show: caption-style-open on:caption "Press Aa, then choose a Font, a Size and a Height in frame"}
3. Set the colour of the [Words] and the [Outline]. {show: caption-style-open on:caption "Press Aa, then set the colour of the Words and the Outline"}
4. Add a [Shadow] or change the [Letter spacing]. {show: caption-style-open on:caption "Press Aa, then add a Shadow or change the Letter spacing"}

## Details

The style is for every caption in the sequence: the size, the colour and where on the frame the
captions sit. Colour is your own choice rather than a fixed theme colour, because captions are
part of the picture and not part of the app's interface.

[Font] lists the four that come with the app first, Montserrat ExtraBold, Anton, Bebas Neue and
Poppins Black, then every font installed on this computer. The four that come with the app look
the same on any computer. A font of your own exports the same on this one; carried to a computer
that does not have it, the captions still break into lines in the same places, but the export
draws them in another font and says so when it finishes. Texts can use the same four fonts, at
the top of their font list.

[Words] is the colour of every word but the one being said, and [Outline] its thickness, from
None to Thick, and colour.

[Shadow] puts a copy of the words behind them, down and to the right, from None to Far, in the
colour beside it. [Letter spacing] sets the letters closer or further apart, from Tight to Wider.
The Output view and the export draw both the same.
