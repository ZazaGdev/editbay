Make captions stand out from a busy picture, and the spoken word stand out from the rest.

## Steps

1. Press [Aa] on the Captions track to open How captions look. {show: caption-style-open on:caption}
2. Tick [Box behind the words] and set [Box opacity]. {show: caption-style-open on:caption "Press Aa, then tick Box behind the words"}
3. Or set [Glow] to Soft or Strong. {show: caption-style-open on:caption "Press Aa, then set Glow"}
4. Under Word being said pops, tick [Bigger], [Boxed] or both. {show: caption-style-open on:caption "Press Aa, then tick Bigger or Boxed under Word being said pops"}

## Details

[Box behind the words] puts a rounded box behind the whole caption, in the colour beside it, with
[Box opacity] to let the picture show through. [Glow] blurs a halo round the letters, Soft or
Strong, in its own colour.

**Word being said pops** makes the word being spoken stand out. [Bigger] draws it a quarter
larger, the rest of its line making room; [Boxed] puts a box of its own colour behind it. Tick
either or both. The box behind the caption stays the same size as the words take turns to grow,
so it does not twitch.

These are part of the caption style, so they apply to every caption in the sequence.
