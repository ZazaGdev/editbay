Keep a caption look under a name, and give it to every sequence in one go.

## Steps

1. Press [Aa] on the Captions track to open How captions look. {show: caption-style-open on:caption}
2. Press [Save as my style] to keep the look under a name. {show: caption-style-open on:caption "Press Aa, then Save as my style"}
3. In any project, press the style's button above it to use it.
4. Press [Apply to all sequences] to give every sequence this look. {show: caption-style-open on:caption "Press Aa, then Apply to all sequences at the bottom"}

## Details

[Save as my style] keeps the whole look under a name: font, colours, outline, shadow, spacing,
box, glow and pop, but not where the captions sit. Your styles show as buttons above it in every
project. Press one to give this sequence's captions that look, in one undo; the x beside it
forgets it. Caption styles and text styles are kept apart. [Settings], under [Styles], lists
every saved style to rename or forget.

The style is for every caption in the sequence. [Apply to all sequences], at the bottom of How
captions look, gives the captions of every other sequence in the project the same look, once you
say you are sure: each keeps where its captions sit. One undo puts them back.
