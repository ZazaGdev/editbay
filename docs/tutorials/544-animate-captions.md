Make captions come in and go out with a fade, a pop or a slide, a whole line at a time or each word as it is said.

## Steps

1. Press [Aa] on the Captions track to open How captions look. {show: caption-style-open on:caption}
2. Set [Animation] to Fade, Pop, Slide up or Slide down. {show: caption-style-open on:caption "Press Aa, then set Animation"}
3. Pick [Whole line] or [Word by word]. {show: caption-style-open on:caption "Press Aa, then pick Whole line or Word by word"}

## Details

**Fade** fades a caption in and out. **Pop** grows it in from smaller, a little past its size,
and settles; it shrinks away as it goes. **Slide up** rises into place and carries on up as it
leaves, and **Slide down** drops in and carries on down. **None** cuts captions in and out, as
they always were.

[Whole line] brings the whole caption in at once. [Word by word] shows each word only once it is
said, coming in with the animation, while the word being said keeps its highlight colour; the
caption leaves as a whole.

Each animation has its own length, a quarter of a second or so, and takes at most a third of a
short caption, so it is still read standing still. The export draws the same frames as the
preview. Like the rest of the caption style, it applies to every caption in the sequence.
