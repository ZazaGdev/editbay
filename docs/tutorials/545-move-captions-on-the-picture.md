Put one caption, or all of them, anywhere on the frame.

## Steps

1. To move one caption, double click it on the Output view and drag its box. {show: caption-box or:monitor-view on:caption "Double click the caption on the Output view, then drag its box"}
2. Press `Escape` when it is where you want it.
3. To move them all, press [Relocate] under How captions look. {show: caption-style-open on:caption "Press Aa, then Relocate"}
4. Drag the caption on the Output view. {show: caption-box or:monitor-view on:caption "Drag the caption on the Output view, and every caption that follows the style moves with it"}

## Details

**Double click a caption on the Output view** to move that one on its own: a box appears round
it, and dragging the box puts it anywhere on the frame. Escape when it is where you want it. The
Inspector then says it was moved by hand, and [Follow the style] puts it back with the rest.

[Relocate], under How captions look, moves every caption that follows the style at once: press
it, then drag the caption on the Output view. Captions you moved by hand stay where you put them,
unless [Include captions you moved by hand] is ticked: the box round such a caption is drawn
fainter and says so. A double click on it turns Relocate off and moves that one alone.

Each drag is one undo step, and the export puts every caption exactly where the Output view
shows it.

Use the safe zone guides, `G`, to keep captions clear of the buttons TikTok, Reels and Shorts
draw over your video.
