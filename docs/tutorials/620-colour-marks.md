Flag a take with a colour so it is easy to find again in a long timeline.

## Steps

1. Right click a clip on the timeline. {show: clip on:picture}
2. Under Mark, pick one of the five colours. {show: clip on:picture "Right click this clip, then pick a colour under Mark"}
3. To take it off, pick the same colour again.

## Details

The colour shows as a band on the clip **and on the overview strip**, so a take you flagged is
easy to find again in a long timeline.

The mark goes on the linked sound too, so the pair stay recognisable together.

There are five colours and no more: Red, Amber, Green, Blue and Purple. The names describe what
the colour looks like rather than what it means, because a name like "Reshoot" would be wrong for
half the marks anyone actually makes. A mark means whatever you decide it means.

Marks are different from markers: a mark colours a clip, a marker is a flag at a moment on the
sequence. Use a mark for "this take is good" and a marker for "something happens here".
