Small marks on a clip's name strip say what has been done to it, and open the settings for it.

## Steps

1. Hover a mark on a clip's name strip to read what it is. {show: clip-mark-effect or:clip-effects on:marked "Hover the marks on a clip to read what they are"}
2. Click the mark to open its section in the Inspector. {show: clip-mark-effect or:clip-effects on:marked "Click a mark to open its section in the Inspector"}

## Details

A clip that has been worked on says so on its name strip, with a small mark for each kind of
change:

- **Zoom**: the picture zooms or moves over the clip.
- **Look**: a colour look, the colour sliders moved, or a blur.
- **Framing**: split, picture in picture, placed in the frame, a frame round it, or blurred bars.
- **Sound**: noise cleaned up, the voice enhanced, or a volume other than 100%.

On a clip too short for them all they become one **fx** and a count, and hovering that lists
them.

**Click a mark** to select the clip and open the Inspector section it is about, scrolled into
view. It opens even a section you folded or hid, since you asked for it. The one **fx** opens
the first.
