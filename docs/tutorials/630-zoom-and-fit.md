See the whole sequence at once, or get close enough to cut on an exact frame.

## Steps

1. Press [Fit], or `Shift+F`, to fit the whole sequence on screen. {show: timeline-fit}
2. Press `=` and `-`, or the zoom buttons, to zoom in and out. {show: zoom-in}
3. Hold `Ctrl` and turn the mouse wheel to zoom around the pointer.
4. Press the button beside [Fit] to step the tracks through three heights. {show: track-size}

## Details

The strip above the ruler shows the **whole sequence** with a box around the part you are
currently looking at. It is the fastest way to know where you are in a long timeline. [ALL]
shows or hides it.

Zoomed all the way in, one frame is 48 pixels wide: the ruler labels every few frames and marks
each frame's edge, for cuts and trims on an exact frame.

`Ctrl` with the mouse wheel zooms around the pointer, which keeps what you are looking at under
the cursor instead of sliding away. A quick spin of the wheel zooms by every notch it turned, and a
smooth wheel or a touchpad stays on the same moment however many small steps it sends. Only the
start and end of the timeline stop it, since it cannot scroll past them.

Taller tracks show more waveform and filmstrip, shorter ones fit more tracks on screen at once.

None of this is an edit, so zooming and resizing never appear in your undo history.
