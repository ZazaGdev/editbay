Take only the part of a long recording you want, instead of all of it.

## Steps

1. Press the [...] button on a source card, or right click the card. {show: asset-more on:asset}
2. Choose [Skim]. {show: asset-more on:asset "Press this, then choose Skim"}
3. Move the pointer across the strip, and press `I` where the good part starts and `O` where it ends.
4. Press [Add the marked], and only that part goes onto the timeline.

## Details

Every source card has a three dots button beside its name, and everything you can do with that
recording is behind it: [Skim], [Add to timeline], [Show in folder] and [Remove from bin].
Right clicking the card opens the same menu.

[Skim] opens the recording as a strip. Move the pointer across it and the picture follows. Mark
the good part with `I` and `O`, then add it, and only the marked part goes onto the timeline.

This is worth the habit. Bringing in the whole of a forty minute recording and cutting it down
afterwards is much slower than taking the three minutes you want in the first place.

The same skimming works on clips already on the timeline: move the pointer across one without
pressing, and the picture follows while the playhead stays where it is.
