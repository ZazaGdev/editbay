Export one stretch of the sequence instead of the whole of it.

## Steps

1. Mark the range with `Shift+I` and `Shift+O`, or select the clips. {show: clip on:picture}
2. Press [Export...] in the top bar. {show: open-export}
3. Under Part, choose [The marked range] or [What the selected clips cover]. {show: open-export "Open Export, then choose the part under Part"}

## Details

**Only part of it** goes under Part: the range marked with `Shift+I` and `Shift+O`, or the
stretch the selected clips cover, from the first one's start to the last one's end. [The whole
sequence] exports all of it, as usual.

The part chosen is used by the transcript too, including [Save only the transcript (.txt)].
