Save the frame at the playhead as a picture, for a cover image or a thumbnail.

## Steps

1. Move the playhead to the frame you want. {show: playhead-head}
2. Press `Ctrl+Alt+S`.

## Details

For a cover image, `Ctrl+Alt+S` saves the frame at the playhead as a PNG in the export folder,
from anywhere in the app, with no need to open Export.
